Video & Motion Design

Alongside interface design, I have produced a range of promotional, demonstration and instructional videos supporting products, marketing campaigns and customer communication.

These projects often combined design, storytelling, motion graphics, editing and user education.

Why Motion Matters

Why Motion Matters

Motion can be an effective tool for explaining products, demonstrating interactions and communicating complex ideas.

Throughout a variety of projects I used video to:

  • Demonstrate product concepts
  • Support product launches
  • Explain workflows
  • Improve customer understanding
  • Create promotional content
